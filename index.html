Qinrui Xiahou 夏侯沁蕊

I am a postdoctoral fellow jointly appointed at the HKU Business School and the HKU Centre for AI, Management and Organization (CAMO). I use large-scale field experiments to study corporate finance and organizational behavior. My current projects examine how firms and markets respond to investor demand for ESG, how governance structures shape supply chains, and how belief calibration and AI assistance affect workers’ decisions and earnings.

I have published seven articles in scientific and economics journals. My research is supported by grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China (as sole principal investigator) and MIT J-PAL (as co-principal investigator). I am a recipient of the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship, the HKU Presidential PhD Scholarship, and HKU’s Research Postgraduate Student Innovation Award and Research Progress Award.

Before joining HKU, I worked as a Research Analyst at Resources for the Future in Washington, D.C., a Teaching Fellow in Yale’s Department of Economics, and a Student Research Fellow at the Yale Hixon Center for Urban Ecology. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from HKU Business School, a Master of Environmental Science from Yale University, and a Bachelor of Economics from Renmin University of China.
