Qinrui Xiahou 夏侯沁蕊

I am an incoming postdoctoral fellow jointly appointed at the HKU Business School and the HKU Centre for AI, Management and Organization (CAMO). My research uses large-scale field experiments to study corporate finance and organizational behavior.

My current projects examine firms’ and markets’ responses to investor ESG demand, supply chain governance, and how belief calibration and AI assistance affect workers’ decisions and earnings. I completed my Ph.D. in Economics at the University of Hong Kong on September 24, 2026.

Research interests

Environmental, development, and organizational economics, with a focus on using field experiments for causal inference.
